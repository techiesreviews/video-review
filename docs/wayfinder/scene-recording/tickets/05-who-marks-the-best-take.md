## Question

When is "best" chosen: right after the take (a star on the Prompter), after the session while watching, or
on the review page? Can there be more than one best, and can the choice change later? Does the computer
suggest one (for example the last complete take, as the cutter does today) for Lex to confirm?

## Resolution (2026-11-13)

Lex chose the star layout in [Pick the Prompter layout](02-pick-the-prompter-layout.md); his answers on the computer's
part: "4. the computer can also help pick it's favorite", then "5. yes / 6. yes / 7. yes".

- Lex decides the best take, at any time: on the Prompter's left rail (an older take) or on the review page. The
  newest take carries the star by default.
- The computer also picks a favorite. It uses free checks on the sound only: a clean end, a length close to the
  other takes, and no long pauses in the middle. Comparing with the script through a transcript uses paid
  HappyScribe minutes, so it is switched on per video only when Lex asks.
- The computer's favorite shows only on the review page, as a hollow star beside Lex's gold one. The Prompter
  shows nothing extra.
- For the rough cut, a scene Lex has not starred uses the newest take. If the computer thinks the newest is
  clearly worse, the better take goes in and the scene is flagged for Lex (his rule of 2026-11-10: last complete
  take wins unless clearly worse). A gold star from Lex always wins.
